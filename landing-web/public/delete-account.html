🗑️ AstroGuru Account & Data Deletion Policy

Google Play Data Safety Compliance: AstroGuru (com.astroguru.app) provides transparent mechanisms for users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data.

⚠️ Irreversible Action: When you delete your account, your personal profile, saved Kundli birth charts, consultation transcripts, audio messages, and wallet balance will be permanently purged from our systems.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your account instantly from within the mobile application without waiting:

  1. Open the AstroGuru app on your Android or iOS device.
  2. Tap the Settings gear icon on the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Session section.
  4. Tap "🗑️ Delete Account & Wipe Data".
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account, local cache, and cloud data will be immediately erased.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request manual data deletion via email:

  • Send an email to: support@astroguru.app
  • Subject Line: Request for Permanent Account Deletion - [Your Registered Phone/Email]
  • Verification: Include your registered mobile number or email address used during registration.
  • Turnaround SLA: All manual deletion requests are verified and completed within 48 hours of receipt. Confirmation will be sent upon completion.
✉️ Email Deletion Request to support@astroguru.app

What Data Is Deleted?

  • Personal details (Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, City of Birth).
  • Astrological calculation charts (Lagna, Kundli, Navamsha, Dasha logs).
  • Chat transcripts, audio messages, and consultation notes with astrologers.
  • Wallet balance ledger and transaction histories.
  • Authentication tokens and FCM push notification tokens.
