Effective Date: August 09, 2026 | Latest Revision: September 20, 2026 | Package ID: com.astroguru.app

Data Privacy Commitment: AstroGuru is deeply committed to safeguarding your personal information, birth details, and spiritual privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party ad networks or brokers.

1. Introduction

AstroGuru ("we", "our", or "us") provides high-precision Vedic Kundli calculations, daily Rashiphal predictions, spiritual remedies, and live consultations with certified Acharyas. This Privacy Policy governs all data collected across the AstroGuru mobile app (Android/iOS) and web portals.

2. Information We Collect

  • Vedic Birth Profile: Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Time of Birth, and City of Birth (used strictly on-device and in accordance with Swiss Ephemeris calculations to chart Janam Kundli, Dasha timelines, and 36-Gun Milan matching).
  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number or email address used for secure OTP / Firebase authentication.
  • Consultation Messages & Audio: Text messages and optional audio voice notes shared with astrologers during active consultation sessions to facilitate astrological guidance.
  • Device & Crash Telemetry: Standard diagnostic data (operating system version, device model) to ensure crash-free execution and reliable notification delivery.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To calculate accurate Vedic planetary positions, Lagna, Rashi, and Nakshatras.
  • To deliver daily Shubh Muhurat and Rahu Kaal push notifications.
  • To process wallet balance top-ups and fulfill consecrated temple puja bookings.
  • To prevent fraudulent chargebacks or unauthorized access.

4. Microphone & Audio Permissions

The AstroGuru mobile app requests microphone access (RECORD_AUDIO) solely when the user chooses to record a voice note during an astrologer consultation. Audio is never recorded in the background or used for ad targeting.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

All client-server communications are encrypted via industry-standard TLS 1.3/HTTPS encryption. On-device data is secured using hardware-backed SecureStore and AES encryption. You may retain your account as long as desired or request immediate deletion at any time.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to view, modify, or permanently delete your account and all associated data. You can delete your account directly inside the app under Settings → Account Session → Delete Account & Wipe Data, or by submitting a web request at AstroGuru Account Deletion Page.
